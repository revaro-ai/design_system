# ListRow

One row in a list: stock, chats, settings. Something on the left to recognise it, a title and one line of detail, and a status or arrow on the right.

The leading slot holds a product photo, a customer's initials or an icon. The trailing slot holds the one thing the seller needs to see without opening the row: a stock pill, a status badge or a chevron.

- 56px minimum height; the whole row is the tap target.
- Titles and details truncate to one line each. If it needs more, it belongs in the detail screen.
- One badge on the right at most. The row's job is to help her decide whether to open it.
