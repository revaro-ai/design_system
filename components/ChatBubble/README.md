# ChatBubble

Shows a conversation so the seller can see straight away who said what: the customer, Revaro, or herself.

Customer on the left in `bubble-customer`. Revaro on the right in slate, with "Revaro · automatic" under it. The seller on the right in a terracotta tint, with "You". Handovers and pauses go in a small centred note (`rv-chat__notice`), e.g. "Handed to you: refund question".

Revaro's bubble always carries its label. This is the one place where a seller could mistake an automatic reply for something she said, and that mistake would cost us her trust.

Put `lang="my"` and `rv-my` on Burmese text so it gets Pyidaungsu and the taller line height.
