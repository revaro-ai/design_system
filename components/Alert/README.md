# Alert

Tells the seller something about how Revaro is working right now, and what she can do about it.

Bold first line: what happened. Second line: what to do. No technical words; "Reconnect your Page", never "token expired".

`info` for something she changed or should know (Revaro paused here). `warning` for working but worse (stock count is old). `danger` for broken until fixed (can't reach Messenger). One alert per screen, worst first. They stay until the problem is gone; they don't fade out on their own.
